Is my medicine order confirmed when I submit the form?＋

No. The form prepares a request for you to send through WhatsApp. Your request is confirmed only after the shop checks availability and accepts it.

Yes. Choose Doorstep Delivery, then use GPS, select a map pin, or enter your address. Home delivery is available every day from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM. The shop will confirm delivery coverage and acceptance.

Yes. You can send your request to either 9989105144 or 9985358726 using the buttons above, or call either number directly.
